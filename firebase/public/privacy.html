Who we are

SBS Beauty Spa ("we", "us") is a beauty business in Sunninghill, Sandton, Gauteng, South Africa. This policy explains what personal information the SBS Beauty Spa app ("the app") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We process personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

How we use your information

  • To provide the services you ask for: bookings, shop orders, payments and delivery.
  • To send service messages: booking confirmations, receipts, reminders, delivery updates and login codes, by in-app notification, email and, if enabled, push notification.
  • To keep accounts secure and to prevent fraud and misuse.
  • To understand how the business is doing, using totals such as revenue and number of bookings. These reports are for our team only.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting duties.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing to third parties.

Who we share it with

We share only what each provider needs to do its job. These providers process information on our behalf:

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud): account sign-in, our database, file storage, server functions and push notifications.
  • Google Places: when you type a delivery address, the text you type is sent to Google to suggest matching addresses.
  • Paystack: to take card and other payments securely.
  • The Courier Guy (Shiplogic): your name, phone number, delivery address and parcel details, so your shop order can be collected, delivered and tracked.
  • Twilio SendGrid: to send emails such as login codes, confirmations and receipts.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our customers or the public from harm.

Where your information is stored

Our providers run data centres in several countries, including outside South Africa. We choose reputable providers and use them only for the purposes above.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Access to customer records is limited to authorised staff, and sign-in to the app requires a password and an emailed code. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and fix problems quickly.

How long we keep it

We keep your account details while your account is active. Records of bookings, orders and payments are kept for as long as the law requires for tax and accounting, which is generally up to five years. Login codes expire after 10 minutes and are not kept once used.

Your rights and deleting your account

You may ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you may object to our processing. To delete your account and associated personal information, email structsbsbeauty@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm and carry out your request within 30 days. We may need to keep payment and order records we are legally required to retain.

You can turn push notifications off at any time in the app (Profile, then Notifications) or in your phone's settings.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to South Africa's Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app.
