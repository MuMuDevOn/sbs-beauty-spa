Common questions

  • How do I book? Open the app, choose a service, pick a date and time, and pay your deposit.
  • How do I change or cancel a booking? Go to Booking History in the app. Rescheduling follows the salon's time rules shown in the app.
  • I did not receive my login code. Check your spam folder, then try logging in again. If it still does not arrive, email us.
  • How do I delete my account and data? Email us from your account's email address and we will remove your account.
  • Where is my order? Open Purchase History in the app and use Track Order.
