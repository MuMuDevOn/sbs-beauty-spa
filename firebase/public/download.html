Android 7.0 or newer · about 40-100 MB

How to install

  1. Tap the download button on your Android phone.
  2. When the download finishes, open the file.
  3. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then go back and tap Install.
  4. Open SBS Beauty Spa, create an account and log in.

Google Play Protect may show a warning because the app is not installed from the Play Store. Choose Install anyway if you trust this page.
